"Folio" means two book pages per side of a sheet of printer paper, or four book pages total per sheet. You will print out the PDFs in the "folio" folder on grain short paper, double-sided, flip on short side. Your final text block will be A5.

"Quarto" means four book pages per side of a sheet of printer paper, or eight book pages total per sheet. You will print out the PDFs in the "quarto" folder on grain long paper, double-sided, flip on short side. Then you'll cut each A4 sheet in half crosswise. Your final text block will be A6.
